# Rapidplay publishing assets

The icon source is the user-selected rapidplay-creative-clean-transparent.png copied unchanged from the original project's icon-concepts folder to store/icon-source.png on October 13, 2026. This replaces the earlier rapidplay-creative.png with the supplied cleaner transparent edges. No independent authorship or third-party license claim is made for that supplied asset. Runtime icons (16, 32, 48, 128), the 128×128 store icon, the 256×256 website mark, and the promotional tile all derive from this same source. The 440×280 tile retains its locally rendered brand text and dark background.

The source is a 1254×1254 RGBA PNG; SHA-256: c9ccc7a8501e253cc9e495fcfa47b93440379c1c80929eba96c651afd62146d0. Sized assets use Pillow 12.3.0 Image.Resampling.LANCZOS with the full source canvas and preserved alpha. The store icon is byte-identical to extension/icons/icon128.png. The tile composites a 180×180 resized source at (130, 12) over its existing background.

Store screenshots are new 1280×800 captures of the actual installed Rapidplay extension in Chromium. The demonstration page and synthetic media are generated locally for this project; no third-party player branding, footage, account data, or mocked extension controls are included. Screenshots depict the panel, scope, settings, shortcut recording, and closed-panel speed toast.

The original project's artwork alternatives, screenshots, promotional video, and SVG marks are omitted. Assets in store/ and docs/ are publication materials; only extension/ goes into the Web Store ZIP.
