# Rapidplay publishing assets

The icon source is the user-selected rapidplay-creative.png copied unchanged from the original project's icon-concepts folder. No independent authorship or third-party license claim is made for that supplied asset. Runtime icons (16, 32, 48, 128), the store icon, website mark, and promotional tile all derive from this same source. The 440×280 tile adds locally rendered brand text on a dark background.

Store screenshots are new 1280×800 captures of the actual installed Rapidplay extension in Chromium. The demonstration page and synthetic media are generated locally for this project; no third-party player branding, footage, account data, or mocked extension controls are included. Screenshots depict the panel, scope, settings, shortcut recording, and closed-panel speed toast.

The original project's artwork alternatives, screenshots, promotional video, and SVG marks are omitted. Assets in store/ and docs/ are publication materials; only extension/ goes into the Web Store ZIP.
